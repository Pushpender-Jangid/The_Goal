(A) \(\angle P=60^\circ\)

Case Study Based Questions

21. Window Pattern – Similarity & Trisection
4 Marks
A triangular window of a building represents a triangle \(\triangle ABC\) with \[ \angle A=90^\circ \] and \[ AB=AC. \] Points \(P\) and \(R\) trisect \(AB\) and \[ PQ\parallel RS\parallel AC. \]
(i) Show that \[ \triangle BPQ\sim\triangle BAC. \]
(ii) Prove that \[ PQ=\frac13 AC. \]
(iii) If \[ AB=3\text{ m}, \] find the lengths of \(BQ\) and \(BS\). OR Prove that \[ BR^2+RS^2=\frac49BC^2. \]
Set 430/6/1
22. Fine Arts Design – Equilateral Triangle
4 Marks

(i) Determine the length of \[ OB. \]
(ii) Is \[ DE\parallel CA? \] Give a reason.
(iii) Write all the angles of quadrilateral \(OEBD\) and show that it is a cyclic quadrilateral. OR Find the perimeter of \[ \triangle ABC. \]
