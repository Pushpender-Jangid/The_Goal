II

Assertion – Reason Question

7. Consistency and Infinitely Many Solutions
1 Mark
Assertion (A): The value of \(p\) for which the system of equations \[ 4x+py+8=0 \] and \[ 2x+2y+2=0 \] is consistent is \(4\).
Reason (R): The system of equations \[ a_1x+b_1y=c_1 \] and \[ a_2x+b_2y=c_2 \] is consistent with infinitely many solutions if \[ \frac{a_1}{a_2} = \frac{b_1}{b_2} = \frac{c_1}{c_2}. \]
Set 430/1/1
