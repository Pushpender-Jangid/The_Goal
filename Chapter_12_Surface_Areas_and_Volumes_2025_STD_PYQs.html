Section A: Assertion–Reason — 1 Mark

5. 1 Mark
Series 30/1/1 (Q20) & 30/1/3 (Q20)
Assertion (A): If we join two hemispheres of same radius along their bases, then we get a sphere.

Reason (R): Total Surface Area of a sphere of radius r is 3πr².

Note: Assertion is true, but Reason is false as the total surface area of a sphere is 4πr².
